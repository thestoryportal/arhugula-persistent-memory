R2 executor startup, one sequence: the coordinator spawns the executor suspended (posix_spawn with POSIX_SPAWN_START_SUSPENDED). It enrolls the executor's exact identity (pid, start time, audit token, unit-profile hash, sandbox-profile hash). It then itself commits CONSUME_UNIT naming that enrolled identity (actor = coordinator, subject = executor), commits the unit's durable ACTIVE, and only then sends SIGCONT. CONSUME_UNIT leaves the EXECUTOR role's allowlist and becomes a coordinator-internal transition with its own receipt. On first run, before opening any memit path, the executor confirms by QUERY that its unit is ACTIVE for its own enrolled identity, and exits nonzero otherwise. Why: no executor instruction runs before durable ACTIVE, which keeps C:59's order without the executor consuming for itself.
R3 recovery observer, independent of the coordinator: MAESTRO launches O `["observe","--scope","RECOVERY",...]` directly from a hash-pinned recovery launch profile under its own launchd label, never through coordinator IPC. It authenticates as MAESTRO's local UID plus a recovery key file readable only by that UID. It enrolls by exclusive-create of a record in R`recovery/` (a journal the coordinator never writes and that grants nothing). Its sandbox denies every write to authority storage and to memit, and it cannot issue REOPEN or any authority request. It reads process tables, sockets and authority storage read-only. An unreadable or locked store is recorded as a finding, not a wait. Its raw captures are evidence, which MAESTRO commits to git. Replacement authority still needs its closure proof plus MAESTRO's REOPEN under F1 §7, so it works with the coordinator dead, blocked or its storage unusable and never creates a second authority writer.
