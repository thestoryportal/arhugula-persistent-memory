## R1. Git records, Drive bulk, no Intel route to memit

The selected direction is GitHub Git for research records, Google Drive for bulk, and M3-only memit (`B/r2_redesign_operator_ruling.md:7`). Recorded artifact policy puts JSON, manifests, hashes, summaries, CORPUS, ledgers and code in Git; irreplaceable bulk goes to Drive (`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md:272–275`).

PROPOSED data path: M3 publication → approved GitHub origin → Intel pinned checkout → rebuildable research views; M3 completed bulk → M3-initiated cable push → bounded Intel-local checkpoint → Intel upload/verification → verified Drive generation → Intel-local research retrieval (`B/r2_redesign_operator_r8_answers.md:16–22`). Intel never opens memit directly or through a proxy, even when M3 is idle. Research views retain source commit/path/hash and coverage; they cannot manufacture missing evidence. Preserve the separate publication and MathLab authority requirements (`B/integration_plan.md:28`).
PROPOSED Git acquisition: fetch only the reviewed GitHub origin, select an explicitly published commit, verify required manifests and hashes, and keep writable builder copies separate from protected records. A missing/unpublished record is labelled unavailable at that revision; no fallback to an M3 checkout or memit. Pin each query/publication to its actual source commit; update derived views as a paired generation. Intel remains prohibited from local inference, editing, training and embeddings (`B/decisions_log.md:109–111`).
PROPOSED bulk acquisition: request a manifest identity from Git, fetch only its exact verified Drive object/version into budgeted Intel-local storage, compare size and SHA-256, and publish locally only on full match. A partial download stays incomplete. Absence, auth failure or capacity refusal leaves the research request waiting; Intel's own queries/builds continue on labelled available records.

PROPOSED enforcement: physically retain memit on M3; withdraw every memit export, file-service endpoint, disk-access SSH/SFTP/SCP credential, forwarding route and old R2 ingress capability available to Intel. Disable automatic mounts/reconnects. Only M3 initiates the cable connection, sending selected bytes into the approved area on Intel’s own disk; Intel receives copies, never memit handles or access. Intel holds no login, key, share or mount reaching M3, and the connection exposes no reverse file service, shell or forwarding route. Replace legacy checkpoint senders only with this reviewed push (`B/r2_redesign_operator_r8_answers.md:7,16,21–23`).
PROPOSED dashboard remains telemetry only: M3 independently publishes bounded status from internal control storage through the separately reviewed off-memit publication path; Intel reads the published status without a login reaching M3. No Intel-supplied source paths, arbitrary commands or disk handles pass through it. Reconcile the advisory-only dashboard interface (`B/dashboard_host_status_record_v3.md:93,261–265`) with answer 1’s boundary (`B/r2_redesign_operator_r8_answers.md:7`).
**Test:** from Intel, Git fetch and a known approved Drive-object fetch succeed; attempted M3 login, memit mount/share/SSH/SFTP/SCP/proxy/symlink and retained old R2 routes fail before source bytes or handles return, including while an M3 push is connected. The approved M3-initiated push writes only Intel-local paths; reverse requests/forwarding cannot reach M3 or memit. Inspect credentials, exports, mounts, connection permissions and autostarts on both hosts; repeat after an independently authorized restart or configuration change. A successful route back or uninspectable configuration is FAIL; text searches alone are insufficient.

## R3. Proactive dynamic storage criteria S1–S12

Recorded policy requires a disk budget in every scope/prereg and free space ≥ projected writes + **20 GiB** at dispatch (`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md:275`). HW1 choice 6 records about **107 GB target / 86 GB warning**; amendment 16 leaves memit thresholds unchanged (`B/decisions_log.md:91,191`). Exact **100 GiB target / 80 GiB warning** appear in C and explain those approximate decimal values (`C:123–125`). Retain those exact thresholds while strengthening the target into a forecast/admission floor, as settled by answer 4 (`B/r2_redesign_operator_r8_answers.md:10,24`).

**S1 — Declare and qualify peak writes before queue admission.** Each job declares byte/count calculations and a conservative additional peak `P`, covering outputs, temporary duplication, checkpoints, logs, caches/downloads, verification and failure/retry retention across every path on memit's physical pool. Include an enforced/bounded non-science allowance `O`. Qualification covers a frozen workload/input envelope, including admitted sizes/counts, retry limits, cache states and failure paths, with justified aggregate peak bounds for every admitted case. A passing sample run is corroboration only, not an established upper bound; out-of-envelope work requires renewed qualification before admission. Unknown or unbounded growth blocks dispatch; a monitor cannot repair a missing bound. Qualification must preserve the science baseline and catch hidden cache recomputation/fallback (the ruling explicitly raises this risk: `B/r2_redesign_operator_ruling.md:5`).
**Test:** a workload with a missing frozen envelope, component or justified bound, or outside that envelope, refuses; a passing sample alone cannot qualify it. A qualified maximum-size/failure-path control corroborates the justified aggregate peak and must stay within it. Exceeding the bound fails qualification and prevents further dispatch under that budget.

**S2 — Reserve atomically at actual dispatch.** Under the reservation lock, identify the physical device/pool and read current usable free bytes `F`; require `F >= P + O + R + 20 GiB`, where `R` is other reservations' remaining additional peak. Durably reserve before launching. Count existing bytes in measured use, not again as future growth; consume reservations as allocation occurs. Preserve unresolved commitments until stopped/completed maintenance and its actual output/partial/reservation ledger are reconciled under the lock; a stopped push or root exit alone releases nothing. R2 observation does not prove science descendants absent. Reservations are promises backed by writer exclusion/caps in S4, not extra disk capacity.
**Test:** with other gates satisfied, just below the bound refuses, equality passes this criterion, and simultaneous requests cannot both spend the same bytes. Wrong device, missing measurement, failed persistence or an unresolved unbudgeted commitment refuses before launch.

**S3 — Protect headroom at the planned peak.** Require additionally `F - P - O - R >= 100 GiB`; below that forecast, queue the job and propose recovery before starting. Warning is actual free `<80 GiB`; the target and the separate +20-GiB rule both remain visible. Recovery operations may run below target only with their own safe budgets; they cannot borrow science's reservation or justify new science admission.
**Test:** an otherwise admissible job whose forecast ends below target refuses; exact target passes this criterion. At exactly warning threshold no low-space warning fires; below it the warning fires. Warning/target never executes deletion.

**S4 — Bound all other writers during science.** Builders, installers, Git maintenance, cache warmers, folder-size scans with writes, archive workers and housekeeping cannot make unreserved memit writes. Place support work on Intel/local permitted storage, or pause it before dispatch; internal storage never becomes a bulk archive. Permit only capped control/log writes covered by `O`. Commission protected writer permissions and actual host-supported byte caps before reliance; arbitrary same-user/admin bypass invalidates the operating boundary. Science-created helpers' writes belong to `P`, regardless of executable name. Before activation freeze aggregate allocation/reservation accounting and actual support-writer enforcement, including retained retry outputs and temporary allocate/free/reallocate cycles. Retained bytes remain charged; freeing a temporary allocation cannot release a still-needed future reallocation reservation. Neither cumulative writes nor one observed low-water allocation substitutes for the justified additional peak.
**Test:** start a builder, log burst and cache growth during the science sentinel: forbidden memit writes are denied/held, allowed writes stay inside their aggregate allowance, and the science reservation stays available. Exercise retained retries and temporary allocate/free/reallocate cycles: actual bytes plus outstanding future reservations must never undercharge the frozen peak commitments. Unsupported enforcement or an unbounded writer is FAIL, not an assumed zero budget.

**S5 — Observe dynamically without scanning the corpus.** Sample physical-pool usable free space, remaining reservations and write-rate trends during each run; PROPOSED interval **60 seconds**, to detect drift promptly with a cheap filesystem query. An observation older than that proposed interval is stale. Unknown/stale readings block new writers/jobs and show UNAVAILABLE. Alert and hold support writes on a forecast breach; no live offload, deletion or unreviewed change to science. Reuse the free-byte primitive, which reads `statvfs.f_bavail * f_frsize`; the existing display also reports measurement errors and counts shared disks once (`tools/dashboard_storage.py:141–146,328–337`).
**Test:** controlled declining free space updates forecast/warnings by the next sampling interval; a failed read cannot retain a fresh/healthy label. The collector performs no bulk traversal or maintenance during science and its actual command still passes unchanged admission rules.

**S6 — Forecast the entire admitted queue.** Recompute on every enqueue, budget/source change, completion, measured capacity change and backup outcome. Simulate ordered job peaks and retained outputs, overlapping support reservations, unfinished transfers, required upload windows and protected artifacts. Do not count proposed cleanup, deduplication or an unverified upload as reclaimed space. Publish the earliest job that would miss S2/S3, its deficit and a dated offload/cleanup proposal before admitting its predecessors when those predecessors remove the last feasible maintenance opportunity.
**Test:** a queue whose later retained growth breaches headroom produces the named deficit and a maintenance dependency before the triggering job starts. Reordering/changing budgets recomputes it; unknown queued budgets block that queue's admission, and unapproved deletions contribute no free bytes.

**S7 — Maintenance only between science jobs.** M3 push/capture/hash/source verification, Drive retrieval into memit and cleanup start only after R2’s existing gate passes; pause or stop if science starts or observation fails. Intel upload/hash/readback/retry uses only its own sealed checkpoint and can continue during M3 science. Budget retained partials and bounded batches; leave science’s next reservation intact. Schedule earlier protection for irreplaceable output and finished-data uploads between jobs, consistent with the recorded nightly/earlier-protection schedule (`B/decisions_log.md:79–81`; `B/r2_redesign_operator_r8_answers.md:16–24`). Incomplete copies wait/replan or are redone; science does not wait for their completion.
**Test:** a maintenance request while the gate observes science causes no M3 memit I/O; the same approved request succeeds in a clear window. Science starting during it pauses/stops M3 maintenance, with R2’s accepted observation gap, while Intel-local upload continues. Interrupted work retains incomplete status and cannot consume the next job’s reserved capacity.

**S8 — Propose cleanup; obtain exact deletion approval.** Present paths, measured logical/allocated sizes, scientific holds, rebuildability, retained samples, Git hashes and verified archive evidence. Every deletion needs explicit operator approval of the exact paths, size and method; no threshold or retention expiry grants it. Runbook §3.3 has historical standing-reclaim wording, while §3.3a classifies retention/archive obligations; this redesign follows the task's stricter explicit-approval rule (`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md:259–278`).
**Test:** low space, an expired cache or a completed upload without exact approval causes no deletion. An approved deletion still refuses during science or on changed identity/hold; after permitted execution, only measured reclaimed bytes reduce the forecast deficit.

**S9 — Verify irreplaceable offload before counting protection.** Preserve local originals until remote whole-content verification, path/metadata coverage and SHA-256/size receipts are committed to Git. Distinguish copy completion from permission to remove the source. Irreplaceable bulk's Drive tier is recorded policy (`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md:274`).
**Test:** remote missing/altered/partial bytes, incomplete coverage or absent Git receipt leaves bulk unprotected and local originals retained; a fully verified committed generation becomes protected, but remains undeletable without S8 approval.

**S10 — Forecast archive capacity and upload time too.** Measure current Drive and Intel staging capacity and reserved/history/partial growth before copies/uploads; estimate cable-copy and Intel upload/readback durations from observed host throughput and approved bandwidth. Fit M3 copy windows between jobs; Intel internet work need not fit those windows. Queue maintenance early enough; unavailable Drive/quota/bandwidth or short Intel staging blocks reliance on planned offload. Historical upload speed and first-copy duration are dated planning evidence, not promises (`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md:277`; `B/r2_redesign_operator_r8_answers.md:14–17`).
**Test:** a forecast exceeding current remote/Intel capacity, feasible M3 copy windows or required Intel upload completion time holds the dependent science queue before admission and emits the deficit/action proposal; a running science job never waits for a push. Increasing assumed bandwidth or treating historical free space as live capacity cannot make it pass.

**S11 — Revalidate after maintenance/restart/remount.** Match physical identity, committed manifests, actual free bytes and retained partials; reject stale reservations/old epoch tokens. Reconcile conservatively rather than resetting unknown use to zero. Recheck R2’s existing gate and storage/reservation ledgers before further M3 maintenance or science admission; a restart does not itself qualify data or space and is not a maintenance prerequisite.
**Test:** a transition or independently authorized restart/remount with changed device, stale token, lost ledger or unaccounted partial refuses dispatch until surviving commitments reconcile and the applicable unchanged admission checks pass; no duplicate physical pool is counted as new capacity. These checks do not claim complete science-process absence.

**S12 — Commission the prevention claim, not just alerts.** Prove the S1–S11 admission/enforcement chain on real M3 storage with bounded harmless writes, concurrent support attempts, failed measurements and interrupted maintenance. An accepted maximum-bound run must finish with protected headroom; monitor-triggered science termination is FAIL. No promise of disk-full immunity for unbounded workloads, unauthorized privileged writes or hardware faults: those are outside qualified operation. Design adoption may approve these conditional requirements; activation and operational acceptance remain OPEN until enforcement is demonstrated.
**Test:** the maximum declared job plus maximum allowed support growth completes without disk-full, quota-induced science stop, live offload or live cleanup; all rejected workloads never start. Fixtures alone cannot qualify this claim.
