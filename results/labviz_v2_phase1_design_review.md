### Where the summary minima come from
- **Contrast 1.0**: `paint[]` kind `border-top/right/bottom/left` on `html > body > header > nav > button` (the `aria-current` one; 150 screens), `#show-3d` (model-2d/3d), and the Learn/Evaluations toggle `div > button` (learn, evaluations). Border colour equals the button's own fill `rgb(185,198,255)`. The outside-border measures pass, so this is a measurement artifact, not a visible defect. The real mark failure is `.write-mark` at 2.84 (#5).
- **Target 23 px height**: WebKit native selects `#map-preset`, `#learn-era` and `#layer-pick`, at **phone (390) and narrow (360)**. So criterion 14 fails at phone widths, in WebKit only (25 px at landscape and desktop).

### Page length vs the spec's IA intent
Full-page heights from `layout.scroll_height`:
- `line-c10`: 23,593 desktop / 28,886 phone / 31,421 narrow.
- `evaluations`: 26,458 / 35,224 / 36,653.
- `line-f1-conditions`: 11,175 / 16,708 / 17,269.

The byte budget is met. The structure is not:
- Spec line 336 says "The fault is structural, not cosmetic".
- Line 344 asks for results "grouped by experiment and collapsed".
- Line 1081 replaces the v1 boilerplate evaluations list with "Grouped by line".
- Line 395 names the v1 defect "every earlier experiment with the same boilerplate".

The Evaluations page (`chromium-desktop-evaluations.png`) reproduces that defect: every entry is expanded, each title appears twice (as a tag and as a heading), and the template text is repeated ("Would this help: <title>?", "The recorded limit: … This applies to the tested setup.", "A next step … is not recorded in the scoreboard results."). On the C10 line page (`chromium-desktop-line-c10.png` at y≈9,000–11,400), a 24-card experiment grid is followed by an uncollapsed list of document links whose quotes are raw registry table rows ("“ **C10-band412** | track_c | does widened … ”").

So yes, the length defeats §4A's progressive and organised intent (spec lines 340–353), even though it is not a numbered criterion. It is listed as BLOCKING because it is the operator's stated complaint.
