# Helper-agent grounding reports

Four read-only helper agents summarised parts of the grounding set for the lead agent (the lead read the C10 chain, the plan, the inventory, the spec §7–§8, the register, the prior-art sweep and the arc document directly). These files hold the load-bearing content of each helper's hand-back, condensed by the lead; file:line citations are the helper's and were spot-checked where a hypothesis depends on them. Nothing here is evidence; it is an index of what the corpus says.

- `corpus_00_34.md` — CORPUS/00–03 and 04–34: decision ids, verdicts, model-family facts, edited-matrix facts.
- `research_and_specs.md` — every editing method, paper, repository and model named in the surveys and syntheses; multi-token / paraphrase statements; hardware statements.
- `infranodus_arc_raw.md` — the nine ingredients, the 23 evaluation sections, the part-2 title lists, the operator chat inbox, the BLOCKING flags.
- `connected_papers_and_genesis.md` — the Connected Papers collection by keyword group; the genesis artifacts on why in-weight, multi-token and write-engine choices.
- `browser_sources.md` — written when the browser helper returns (Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, Connected Papers, NotebookLM).
