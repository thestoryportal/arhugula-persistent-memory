8. **The block-F replica is bit-identical to r0, so gate A measured repeatability, not robustness.**

   **What was compared.** Every `r3/F_r0_*.json` record was compared with its `r3/F_r1_*.json` counterpart over all JSON leaf values, every float included. The multi record has 3,706 leaves, each mint-1 record (seeds 0, 1, 2) has 6,424, and the modifier records have 3,443, 3,447 and 3,446. The only leaves that differ are `replicate` (0 → 1), `pid` and `when_utc`. The modifier records carry `pid: null` and no `when_utc`, so in them only `replicate` differs. Counts, gates, per-position probabilities, write ratios and locality values are identical in every digit.

   **Why.** The replica's inputs were r0's inputs:
   - Both replicates load the same frozen context set, `r3/contexts_F.json` (`arms.py:769`). It is installed before LAW#5 (`:782`; `set_contexts`, `:173–180`).
   - The mint arms load r0's step-0 init rows and r0's step-0 solved targets `z`, and they write those targets through `Z_OVERRIDE` (`arms.py:770`, `:797–800`, `:816`).
   - The r1 modifier records re-read r0's step-0 admission (`step0_file` = `step0/F_r0_modifier_s*.json` in the r1 records; `arms.py:774`). They are not a second admission.
   - The multi re-solves its targets in-process (`arms.py:818–820`), on the frozen contexts.

   The historical harness has one stochastic input, the context-template sample. `get_context_templates` draws the templates once per process by unseeded top-k multinomial sampling (`memit_dry_run/memit/memit/memit_main.py:304–320`; `memit_dry_run/memit/util/generate.py:116–119`). CORPUS/44 F-A4 (`CORPUS/44_C10L_COVGEOM_SPARSEBAND.md:32`) identified this sampling as the source of cross-process variation. The ±4–5 envelope of CORPUS/45 (`CORPUS/45_C10L_MINT_TOKEN.md:61`: N seed 0 scored 46 and 42 in two processes, G 39 and 38) came from fresh processes that each sampled their own templates. This design froze the templates (§2 :67; :119 lists the freeze as one of this prereg's two new behaviours). With that input fixed, the rest of the computation was deterministic on this machine.

   **Consequence.** :78 specifies gate A as "a fresh-process block on the same frozen context set and the same inits — it measures the ±4–5 process envelope (`CORPUS/45:61`)". The two halves of that sentence are incompatible: the frozen context set and the same inits remove the source of that envelope. Gate A measured zero process variance. The LEAD labels of mint-1(frozen) seeds 0 and 1 are "replicated" only in the sense that the same inputs gave the same outputs. They are not shown to be robust to context resampling or to a second seed draw. :103 already states that a replication on the same inits is not an independent seed draw.

   **Where variation was observed.** Within r3, the seeds differ: mint-1(frozen) scored 46/37/40 (range 9) against the multi reference of 14. Across attempts, each with freshly sampled contexts and the same init rows (row sha256 `2c1d09e7…`, `f50f9ea5…` and `9d5cc3c8…` for seeds 0, 1 and 2, the same in r1, r2 and r3):
   - mint-1(frozen) seed 0 scored 40 (r1), 34 (r2) and 46 (r3); seed 1 scored 36, 35 and 37; seed 2 scored 41, 37 and 40.
   - A7-multi(frozen) para_full was 10, 10 and 14 (ctx sha256 `aa476792…`, `dd73fdc9…`, `8638d12c…`).
   - Modifier seed 0 was admitted at 17/24 in both r2 and r3, but different values were underfit: indices [1, 5, 8, 12, 13, 16, 20] in r2 and [7, 8, 12, 13, 17, 20, 22] in r3.

   The cross-attempt spread for seed 0 is 12, larger than the ±4–5 that gate A was meant to measure. These cross-attempt counts are reported descriptively, as :186 requires. They are not pooled, not used as a replication, and relabel nothing: r1 stays HALTED (:170) and r2 stays HALTED-HARNESS. They are also confounded with the source changes between attempts, since the three attempts' source digests differ.
