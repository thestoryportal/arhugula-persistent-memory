**VERDICT: FIX-FIRST.** The design is sound in its skeleton (both arms, three seeds, non-overlap on block V2, frozen context set, bigram control, string ≥ full). Five rule or record defects need fixing before freeze; none overturns the design.

**The single most important thing.** Rewrite the block-F **SECOND-WORD** clause (`C10U:98`): `max(M1F) − min(MO) < 10` is not a non-overlap form, it is the *most favourable* pair, and it cannot fire on the reference's own distribution. With M1F = CORPUS/46's own N seeds {46, 34, 36} (`CORPUS/46:22`), a modifier-only arm distributed *identically* ({46, 34, 36}) gives max(M1F) − min(MO) = 46 − 34 = 12 ≥ 10 → not SECOND-WORD → MIXED; while a latent 15-point deficit (MO ≈ 25, M1F ≈ 40) passes whenever M1F draws low ({34, 34, 36}) and MO high (31): 34 − 31 = 3 < 10. The clause is both a guaranteed false negative against "MO equals mint-1" and an easy false positive against "MO is well below mint-1" — the exact failure the v1 advisor and Codex item 2 had the author remove from block V2. Replace the second conjunct with the deficit test in non-overlap form, `NOT(min(M1F) − max(MO) ≥ 10)` (mint-1 does not beat MO by the margin), and say that equivalence is not identified at three seeds; the label then reads "gain over the string and no demonstrated deficit to mint-1", which is all three seeds can carry.

3. **The P2 inventory misses the prompts the solve actually feeds: the context templates.** `compute_z:46–50` builds every rewriting prompt as `context.format(prompt) + decode(target_ids[:-1])`, and `compute_ks` uses the same templates (`bandknob:196`, `:219`); the templates are model-generated by top-k = 5 sampling over the full vocabulary (`memit_main.py:304–320`, `util/generate.py:77–117`), so they cannot be AST-extracted and are absent from the 403-prompt inventory (`c10u_mint2_wordlist.py:140–141`) and from `all_harness_prompts` (`lib:178–185`). Two concrete gaps:
   - (a) *Where the block's frozen set is generated.* In the mint driver the spare rows are set before `H.main()` (`c10l_mint_arms.py:102` → `:108`), and the first `get_context_templates` call of a process happens inside LAW#5's `apply_memit_to_model` (`bandknob:463` → `:423`), i.e. the templates are sampled from a model whose 48 minted rows are live. CORPUS/45 found every N token "dead" (rank > 1000 on 14 prompts each, `CORPUS/45:22`, `:45`), so a minted id in the top-5 at one of 50 sampled positions is unlikely, not excluded — and a template that contains a decoded minted id would re-tokenise to that id inside every rewriting prompt of the block. Fix: generate and freeze the block's context set in the A7-multi process (tokenizer and rows untouched) and load it in every later process; the prereg currently says only "the first process of a block" (`C10U:67`).
   - (b) *P2(a) must run over the frozen templates.* Once frozen they are plain strings; add `{context.format(CANON).format(subject)}` for all templates × 24 subjects to P2(a) in every process. Cheap, and it closes the only prompt family the inventory cannot cover. I-INFER.

4. **The -BIGRAM qualifier has no operational form.** `C10U:95`: "the modifier fires under swapped or never-edited subjects on at least as many prompts as under the correct subject". Also unspecified: which "swapped edited subject" (pin a permutation, e.g. subject i+12 mod 24, and for arm B the stated no-shared-head-no-shared-modifier constraint). Pre-register: per head, rate under (ii)+(iii) over 7 prompts vs under (i); arm-level **-BIGRAM** if ≥ k of 24 heads (name k). EVIDENCE-SHOWS on the counts; I-INFER on the remedy.
   - Arm B's sharing gives a sharper control the document does not use.
