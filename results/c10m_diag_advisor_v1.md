Single most important next thing: freeze a v2 before implementation that tightens the Q2 `inj_pre` validity gate and prevents Q1 `DRIFT-DOMINANT` from being read as a useful first-token rescue when constrained-full is still below LEAD.

Issues, in priority order:

1. **Q2 control threshold is too loose for its own rationale.** The prereg says `inj_pre_para ≥ 40/72` is “P6 reference 49/72 minus the process spread,” but CORPUS/44 records per-process spread as roughly ±3–5/72, not −9/72. A same-process control is the right design, but the validity gate should be closer to `inj_pre_para ≥ 44/72`; otherwise a degraded injection setup can still pass and make the post-edit comparison flattering. Citations: `docs/C10M_DIAG_READPARTITION_EDITINJECT_PREREG.md:36`, `CORPUS/44_C10L_COVGEOM_SPARSEBAND.md:23`, `CORPUS/44_C10L_COVGEOM_SPARSEBAND.md:39-41`.

Keep the gain metric, but split it: `DRIFT-LEAD` only if `C ≥ 30/72`, otherwise `DRIFT-PARTIAL`/`MIXED`. Citations: `docs/C10M_DIAG_READPARTITION_EDITINJECT_PREREG.md:36`, `docs/C10M_DIAG_READPARTITION_EDITINJECT_PREREG.md:38-43`, `CORPUS/44_C10L_COVGEOM_SPARSEBAND.md:39`.

But the label should be `CHAIN-PRESENT` unless the high-TF failing subjects are a majority or dominate the missed mass. Citations: `docs/C10M_DIAG_READPARTITION_EDITINJECT_PREREG.md:40-42`, `experiments/track_c/c10e_bandknob.py:134-143`, `experiments/track_c/c10e_bandknob.py:352-367`.
