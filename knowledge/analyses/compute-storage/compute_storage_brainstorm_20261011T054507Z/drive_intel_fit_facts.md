## 1. Composition of the ~287 GB Drive source

Class key:
- **R-pin**: regenerable from a pinned public download (the pin is cited).
- **R-derived**: regenerable by re-deriving it from a pinned download.
- **R-runbook**: the runbook marks it safe to reclaim. It is regenerated by re-running the covariance precompute. A re-run reproduces behaviour, not bytes: "Cross-process bit-exact weight reproduction does NOT hold" (`REPRODUCIBILITY.md:26`).
- **R-git**: regenerable from the GitHub remote for pushed refs (`CLAUDE.md:17`: "`git push origin <branch>` after every commit").
- **DUP**: a byte copy of other content in this same source, shown by SHA-256 (§1b).
- **UNCLEAR**: no plan, runbook or manifest line classifies it.

**No row has a citation that classifies it as irreplaceable.** The policy defines the class (`EXPERIMENT_RUNBOOK.md:274`), and the Intel decision says "The recovery copy holds what cannot be rebuilt" (`decisions_log.md:191`). No inventory assigns files to that class.

Against the 50 GB transfer-staging cap:
- The largest single included file is 6,178,316,608 B (`arhugula-persistent-memory/c10l_mint_N_f16.gguf`), so every file fits in one staging batch.
- The HF cache alone (39.239 GB) fits in one 50 GB batch. Streaming the whole 287.177 GB through 50 GB would take at least ⌈287.177/50⌉ = **6 batches**, and more if any in-flight copy is retained.
- Recovery 250 + staging 50 = 300 GB, which is 12.823 GB more than 287.177. Holding a full copy across both areas would spend staging on recovery data, which the design does not permit: files outside the recovery set use staging (`design.md:261`), and sets are not to be added "as free capacity" (`integration_plan.md:25`).
- De-duplication changes Intel disk use only. The Drive upload still carries 287.177 GB unless the source itself changes (option C).

**What the documents say about batching and about a complete Intel copy:**
- **Choice 11:** "stage and verify an Intel checkpoint, then upload/verify from Intel" (`decisions_log.md:139`). Design phase 3: "Upload and verify that immutable Intel checkpoint against Drive" (`design.md:283`). No document says whether a checkpoint may be a sequence of batches, or whether it must exist whole at once on Intel.
- **Staging area:** "larger tasks use reviewed batching or another destination" (`design.md:161`).
