# Prior-art sweep — model editing since 2026-07 (operator-supplied list, 2026-10-04)

**Source:** 22 distinct papers/sites and 20 repositories supplied by the operator "for current workflow experiments as well as future evolving experiments in order to accomplish the primary objective of using an LLM as persistent memory". Grounded by fetch (arXiv HTML/abs, GitHub READMEs) through four sub-sweeps; numbers are as the pages state them and were not checked against PDFs. Each item is tagged against the program's six live questions:

- **Q1** keying locus / held-out-paraphrase generalisation · **Q2** value-class fragility (multi-token, open-range, coined) · **Q3** long-form metrics (BLEU on the edited prompt vs held-out paraphrase/locality) · **Q4** locality, "editing hurts", sequential collapse, capacity · **Q5** hyperparameter transfer across model scale · **Q6** memory architecture and lifecycle (in-weight vs side-store; write/update/forget/verify).

## 1. Papers

### 1.1 Directly on the program's open questions (READ-NEXT)

- **Reproducibility study of AlphaEdit — Suresh et al., arXiv 2606.26783 v2 (Jul 2026).** Reproduces AlphaEdit and extends it to Llama3.2-1B/3B, **Qwen2.5-3B**, Phi3-3.8B, Gemma2-2B. "Qwen2.5-3B achieves 99.80 % efficacy"; ZsRE paraphrase generalisation "90–96 %" on Qwen/Llama; Phi3 and Gemma2 fail (0.43–48.85 %). Capacity: stable to 2–3k edits, "degrades after ~5,000 edits"; LLaMA3-8B MMLU 0.6 → 0.1 by 10k. Hyperparameters for new architectures "required manual derivation … no systematic methodology provided". Fluency metrics in the AlphaEdit codebase inflated 3–10× by a `generate_fast()` caching bug. **Q5 (the single most on-target item: same model, same family, hyperparameters did not transfer and were hand-derived); Q4; Q1; Q3.** Bearing on Route A: independent confirmation that AlphaEdit's shipped settings do not carry to Qwen2.5-3B, and that a *subject-keyed* AlphaEdit on this model reaches 90–96 % paraphrase generalisation on ZsRE, which sharpens F3 (the Route A port's 0/72 is not "AlphaEdit on 3B cannot generalise").
- **ManiEdit — Liu et al., arXiv 2609.33534 (Sep 2026).** Closed-form `W_down` edit for unstructured text with "semantic pivot" keys chosen by leverage score `kᵀΣ⁻¹k`, energy-weighted penalty `‖ΔW Σ^½‖_F`, recursive null-space alignment. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama3-8B-Instruct; UnKEBench, AKEW, EditEverything; after 500 sequential edits "+7.74 BERTScore, +3.99 ROUGE-L" vs strongest baseline, general accuracy "within 1–2 %", baselines (MOSE, AnyEdit, RECT, EvoEdit) "collapse within 100–200 edits". Evaluates Original / Paraphrase / Sub-question. **Q1 (keys inside the written text, chosen by leverage against Σ — the same covariance geometry the Route A ridge diagnostic measured); Q3 (BERTScore under paraphrase, with downstream locality); Q4; Q5 (λ, ε not tabulated).** The leverage-score key rule is the first principled answer in the lineage to "which position to key"; it is a candidate for the subject-keyed hybrid (option ii).
- **ALOE — Li et al., arXiv 2609.29269 (Sep 2026).** Reframes editing as *address* + *write*: learned semantic addresses from paraphrases and same-subject hard negatives, gated low-rank operator in one MLP layer. Llama-2-7B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B. Efficacy 0.955–0.999, locality 0.981–1.000, **generalisation only 0.217–0.472**; gate fires 0.057 on rephrasings vs 0.860 on originals. **Q1 (the paraphrase failure is shown to be address-side, not write-side — the same decomposition Route A's back-test made: canonical transfer 1.00, paraphrase 0.01); Q4; Q6 (in-weight gated store between MEMIT and a side-store).**
- **ForgetBench — Gu et al., arXiv 2607.26455 (Jul 2026).** Retention under up to 500 sequential edits, eval every 10; metrics edit success, retention, forgetting curve, half-life, AUF, temporal consistency, paraphrase gap, fluency; MEMIT, WISE, AlphaEdit, UltraEdit on Llama-3/3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, DeepSeek-R1-7B. AlphaEdit on Qwen2.5: ES 0.92, retention 0.885, half-life 452, paraphrase gap −0.559 ("rote memorization rather than associative knowledge integration"). **Q4; Q6 (weights as long-term parametric memory with an explicit retention/forgetting lifecycle); Q1.** The metric set is reusable locally for the program's D1/capacity line.
- **Moir — Kwon et al., arXiv 2607.20433 (May 2026).** Replaces the Wikipedia preservation covariance `C = K₀K₀ᵀ` with covariance from model-generated text; OLMo-2-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B; Qwen3-8B after 20,000 AlphaEdit edits keeps GSM8K 79.9 % vs 10.9 % with Wikipedia covariance; MEMIT with Wikipedia "collapses to zero within ~200 edits"; Proposition 1: updates depend on the input distribution only through `C`; ~2 h generation per model. **Q4 (asymmetric collapse: math/code degrade, encyclopedic recall survives); Q5 (covariance provenance is itself a transfer hazard).** Directly actionable: the program regenerates its covariance locally (5 h per band); a self-generated corpus is a cheap, local, pre-registerable variant, and the Route A ridge diagnostic (`memit_ridge_diag.json`: last-token keys sit 37–180× under the covariance ridge) says *which* keys the Wikipedia covariance over-protects.
- **GLIME — Jung et al., arXiv 2609.36748 (Sep 2026).** Lifelong editing with a preference loss, replay buffer and orthogonal gradient projection on MLP layers 4–8; Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct; 3k edits reliability 0.830 / generalisation 0.800 (autoregressive decoding), "scales to 5,000 edits", competitors "sharp collapse around 3,000". Stresses that **teacher-forced scoring "tends to overestimate"** vs autoregressive decoding. **Q1/Q3 (the TF-vs-AD gap is the program's `tf_pertok_cont` vs `para_full` distinction, confirmed externally); Q4.** Repo `ekgus9/GLIME` (§2).
- **Model Merging for Knowledge Editing — Fu et al., arXiv 2506.12384 (Jun 2025).** Robust SFT on one FFN layer then merge with base (α≈0.8, top-20 % deltas); Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct; ZsRE edit success 96.95, generalisation 91.58, portability 39.63 (merged); C-Eval 79.35 vs base 79.57; locality 26.42; ROME/MEMIT/LoRA "poor performance in sequential editing". **Q4; Q6 (an in-weight write mechanism that is not keyed — a local-feasible fallback write engine if the keyed family keeps failing on 3B); Q1.**
- **KnowledgeDebugger — Benz et al., arXiv 2607.01000 (Jul 2026).** GUI over EasyEdit (ROME/PMET/MEMIT on `W_down`), GPT2-XL and Llama-3.1-8B. ROME-edited knowledge "emerges significantly earlier (15 layers)" than natural knowledge; single-token subjects at prompt start trigger collapse with "50× perplexity increases"; causal-trace layer varies across paraphrases (layers 12–20). **Q1 (paraphrases localise to different layers); Q4 (a collapse trigger and a perplexity detector, cheap to port — the program already added a generic-NLL probe in Route A); Q5.** Repo `ebnz/lm-debugger` (§2).
- **The Tokens Remember (Toketive) — Baser et al., arXiv 2609.29045 (Sep 2026).** Non-canonical tokenisations of the same string bypass localized edits/unlearning: "38.6 % of alternative tokenizations bypass the modification", 19–57 % across AlphaEdit, RECT, MEMIT, PRUNE, CoME, LTU; hardening (32 % → 4 %) raised ripple effects "from 1.0× to 8.6×". **Q1 (keys live on token paths; a mechanism for surface-variant non-generalisation, acute for coined multi-token subjects — Q2); Q4.** Cheap local check: tokenise the program's coined subjects and values under alternative segmentations.

### 1.2 Mechanistic or secondary (noted, not queued)

- **DOW-KE — Chen et al., arXiv 2608.16932.** Drops MEMIT's intermediate anchor targets; end-to-end multi-layer `ΔW = R·B` with spectral projections; Llama-3-8B CounterFact efficacy 99.85 / generalisation 96.64 / specificity 78.86; reports a "~33 % gap" of unrealised residuals in anchor-based pipelines. **Q1 (keys at the subject's final token); Q4; Q5 (its spectral threshold τ is the same kind of eigenvalue knob Amendment 1 had to rescale).** Explains why MEMIT-style pipelines under-realise targets; needs full-model backprop per batch.
- **IBKE — Chen et al., arXiv 2512.16227.** Trained information-bottleneck editor (3–7 days on two A6000s); Qwen3-1.7B UniEdit 96.88 %. Not local-feasible, not the closed-form family.
- **SafeEdit / secure code — Sun et al., ISSTA 2026, 2608.06848.** Behaviour editing; one note matters: **down-projection edits (MEMIT's target) collapse function worse than up-projection edits** (Pass@1 38.9 % → 20.5 %) and sequential edits degrade a 3B model (23.1 → 12.8). **Q4.**
- **M³Bench medical VLM editing — Zhu et al., 2607.05310.** Locality collapses under LoRA; memory-based editors show "backbone-dependent hyperparameter sensitivity". **Q4, Q5, Q6**; multimodal, no MEMIT family.
- **model-editing.github.io** (Huang, Wang, Shu; Emory + Amazon/ASU/UNC/Cisco): a group site listing Personalization Editing (UPQA), Behavior Editing (BehaviorBench), HalluEditBench (Efficacy/Generalization/Portability/Locality/Robustness), Editing Attack. **Q3/Q4 (HalluEditBench); Q6 (Personalization Editing is the closest published framing to "LLM as persistent user memory").** Repo `amazon-science/personalization-editing-upqa` (§2).

### 1.3 Batch 2 (READ-NEXT marked ★)

- ★ **Sequential editing breaks evidence arbitration without costing accuracy — Anand et al., arXiv 2609.29587 (Aug 2026).** 1,000 sequential CounterFact edits on **Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct** (Mistral-7B replication) with MEMIT, AlphaEdit, LoRA. "Three of five model-method pairings destroy MMLU to chance (0.25) at 1,000 edits **using published hyperparameters**, while edit success and locality metrics remain clean"; AlphaEdit 0.275 MMLU, MEMIT 0.605; MEMIT mom2 sample count cut 100,000 → 20,000 for tractability; edited weights also degrade a retrieval hybrid (0.592 → 0.454). **Q4 (standard locality passes while general ability collapses); Q5 (published AlphaEdit/MEMIT settings do not transfer even within the Qwen2.5 family — the Route A F2/F6 finding one scale up); Q6.**
- ★ **PRISM Edit — Huang et al., arXiv 2607.11327 v3 (Aug 2026), EMNLP 2026.** Temporal editing with one vector at early MLP layers (L4–8) at the **subject-last token**; causal tracing shows the MLP input there is "nearly invariant across temporal contexts (cosine ≥ 0.97)" — "the editing site cannot discriminate time". **Q1 (the clearest published statement of the keying-locus limit: whatever must vary with context is resolved downstream of the subject key, not at it — the mirror image of Route A's last-token key, which varies with context and so does not generalise); Q6 (update without forgetting the old value); Q5.** Repo `CheerCHuang/PRISM-Edit` (80 GB GPU recommended).
- ★ **KLOD — Jeong et al., arXiv 2608.27839 (Aug 2026).** Fine-tuning edit objective with a logit-odds hinge and KL preservation of the non-target distribution; `W_down` only (layer 22 Llama3-8B, layer 6 Qwen2.5-7B); Llama3-8B CounterFact reliability 99.70 / generalisation 47.37 / locality 44.75; scaled to 150k ZsRE edits. ZsRE has "77.1 % multi-token targets" and a **"Strict Rel." metric requiring all target positions correct**. **Q2 (a strict multi-token reliability metric, which is the program's full-sequence criterion under another name); Q4 (explicit generalisation–locality trade-off over α); Q5 (layer 22 vs 6 across families).** Repo `Hostoday/KLOD` (CUDA).
- ★ **Hybrid-Policy Self-Editing (HPSE) — Liu et al., arXiv 2608.11660 (Aug 2026).** Passage editing; edited models recall passages but cannot decompose them into atomic facts; HPSE self-distils from a privileged in-context state; UnKEBench with 5 atomic facts per passage, MQuAKE-CF-remastered; Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B. **Q3 (held-out *decomposition* probes rather than BLEU on the passage — the metric design the long-form lineage otherwise lacks); Q4; Q6 (in-context state as teacher for in-weight writes).** Repo `lliutianc/hpse` (data on HF, no GPU requirement stated).
- ★ **Selective edit reversal via gated singular-vector shrinkage — Jiang et al., arXiv 2609.02091 (Sep 2026).** Reverses chosen ROME/MEMIT/AlphaEdit edits by SVD gates with KL to a reference; reverse-50 on GPT2-XL 96.00 reversed-set agreement, 74.00 remained set; next-token only (continuation ~54 % persists); degrades at 1,000 edits. **Q6 (the only item with a mechanism and numbers for the "forget" step on locate-then-edit writes); Q2; Q4.**
- **PersonaEdit — Huang et al., arXiv 2608.27816.** ROME/MEMIT/AlphaEdit personalisation on OpinionQA; edits at the subject's last token; AlphaEdit ~89 % train vs 44.67 % test at 200 edits, paraphrase robustness 64–66 %; AlphaEdit projection "~25 hours one-time", 200 edits "~2 hours on H100". **Q1, Q6, Q5** (confirms trends; 8B only). Repo `NYCU-NLP-Lab/PersonaEdit` (CUDA).
- **MO-IKE — Wang et al., arXiv 2608.25100 v2.** In-context editing with an RL-trained retriever; Llama-3.2-3B edit success 87.1 → 91.1, retention 41.0 → 63.4. **Q6 (a side-store comparator at the 3B scale with reliability, paraphrase and retention reported jointly); Q4.** Repo `wmd3i/MO-IKE`.

## 3. What changes now (synthesis; I-INFER where marked)

1. **Route A's F3 and F5 gain external support from three directions.** PRISM (subject-last-token MLP input invariant across contexts, cos ≥ 0.97), ALOE (paraphrase failure is address-side: gates fire 0.057 on rephrasings vs 0.860 on originals), and the AlphaEdit reproducibility study (subject-keyed AlphaEdit on **Qwen2.5-3B** reaches 99.8 % efficacy and 90–96 % ZsRE paraphrase generalisation) together say the generalising locus is the subject token and the failure is in addressing, not writing. Cited in `docs/C10K_ROUTEA_RESULT.md` F5 and in the keying-ablation prereg as the external prior for the SUFFICIENT reading.
2. **F2/F6 (ridge weights do not transfer) are independently reported one scale up:** Anand et al. (AlphaEdit at published settings collapses Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct MMLU to chance at 1,000 edits while locality passes) and Suresh et al. (hyperparameters "required manual derivation" per architecture, Qwen2.5-3B included). Q5 is now a documented property of the method family, not a Route A artefact.
3. **The program's locality battery is too small, and the field says so in two ways:** standard locality passes while MMLU collapses (Anand), and down-projection edits degrade function more than up-projection edits (Sun et al.). Candidates that run locally: Round-Edit/Reverse-Edit (Pitfalls, data-only), ForgetBench (Apache-2.0, offline), a generic-NLL/MMLU-subset probe (already added as `generic_nll` in Route A). Pre-register one before the next in-weight run.
4. **Moir is the one cheap, local change to the exact artefact the program owns** (the covariance cache): self-generated-text covariance instead of Wikipedia. The Route A ridge diagnostic measured why it matters here: last-token keys sit 37–180× under the Wikipedia covariance ridge. If the program keeps a MEMIT-style ridge, the covariance corpus is the knob to pre-register next, not the ridge weight.
5. **Metrics to adopt:** KLOD's strict multi-token reliability (= the program's full-sequence criterion, now with an external name), HPSE's decomposition probes for long-form writes, GLIME's teacher-forced-vs-autoregressive caution (= `tf_pertok_cont` vs `para_full`), ForgetBench's half-life / paraphrase gap.
6. **Lifecycle (Q6):** PRISM (update without overwrite), edit reversal (forget, with a capacity ceiling), Pitfalls Round-Edit (integrity), and Awesome-AI-Memory's five operations give the vocabulary for the e2e map's Layer-6 lifecycle; the field's default is that parametric memory is *not* editable in place, which is exactly the claim F1 tests.
7. **Nothing in the sweep tests a 3B base model with a locate-then-edit method except Suresh et al.; nothing offers a local-faithful long-form editor; EasyEdit now bundles AnyEdit but without MPS.** The program's local, 3B-base, held-out-paraphrase setting remains unoccupied ground.

## 4. Addendum (operator, later on 2026-10-04): behavior-edit, MemoryLLM, MDM-KE

- ★ **Knowledge Editing for Masked Diffusion Language Models — Park & Jo (SNU), EMNLP 2026, arXiv 2606.03924 v2.** Tests locate-then-edit on masked-diffusion LMs against autoregressive pairs (LLaMA-3-8B/LLaDA-8B, **Qwen2.5-7B**/Dream-v0-7B, LLaMA-2-7B/DiffuLLaMA-7B, GPT-2-medium/DiffuGPT-m); CounterFact and Kamel (multi-token). "The same early-to-mid-layer MLP at the last subject token is most effective for both." Single-token efficacy "~0.92–1.00"; **multi-token drops "from 0.92 (N = 1) to 0.27 (N = 4)"** on LLaDA, corrected to 0.73 with partial-mask augmentation, across MEMIT, AlphaEdit, PMET. Code `holi-lab/MDM-KE`. **Q1 (independent confirmation of the last-subject-token / early-mid MLP locus, including on Qwen2.5-7B); Q2 (multi-token targets are where in-weight edits break — the program's value-class finding, seen in another model family); Q5 (7B/8B only).**
- ★ **MemoryLLM — Wang et al., ICML 2024, arXiv 2402.04624 v2; repo `wangyu-ustc/MemoryLLM` (also M+, Feb 2025).** Llama2-7B with a fixed latent memory pool in every layer ("7,680 memory tokens in every layer", "1.066B parameters"); a write concatenates the last K = 256 memory tokens with the layer's hidden states, then drops K tokens and shifts the pool: forgetting is structural, "exponentially forgotten at a rate of K/N", retention after N/K writes "(1 − K/N)^(N/K)" ≈ 1/e. Trained on C4, "8 A100-80GB GPUs for three days". Editing: "79.2 on ZsRE and 75.3 on CounterFactual", above ROME, IKE and fine-tuning; "nearly a million memory updates without observing any performance deterioration". Released MemoryLLM-7B/8B (1.67B memory params), 8B-Chat, MPlus-8B; `model.inject_memory()`, ≥ 16 tokens per injection; tested on H100/CUDA 12.2; MIT. **Q6 — the clearest architectural counterpoint to in-weight editing: writes are forward-pass only (no per-write solve), no sequential collapse, but the pool must be pre-trained, recall is not verifiable, in-place update and selective forgetting do not exist, and knowledge decays to ~37 % after 30 writes by design: a sliding window, not a store. M+ adds a long-term memory to address this. Q4 (bounded capacity by construction; 16-token minimum granularity).** Not stated to run on MPS.
- **BehaviorBench / `baixianghuang/behavior-edit` — Huang et al., AAAI 2026 (oral), "Model Editing as a Double-Edged Sword."** Multi-tier benchmark for editing agent ethical behaviour: yes/no, locality questions, **rephrased questions**, multiple-choice, 2–6-hop reasoning, "global moral impact"; Llama-2-7B-chat, Llama-3-8B-Instruct, OLMo-7B, Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Mistral-7B; "at least one GPU with 48 GB"; MIT. **Q3/Q4 (a tiered held-out metric design beyond edited-prompt scoring, with locality and general-impact tiers); Q1 (rephrased tier); Q5 (7B/8B only).** Content (ethics steering) is off-program.
- **ALOE (2609.29269)** was already in §1.1.

**Bearing (I-INFER):** MDM-KE is the second independent 2026 confirmation (with Suresh et al.) that the subject-last-token locus holds on the Qwen2.5 family and that multi-token values are the fragile class — both of the program's load-bearing findings now have external replications on sibling models. MemoryLLM reframes Q6: a latent memory pool buys cheap writes and no collapse at the price of unverifiable recall and designed forgetting, which is the opposite trade from in-weight editing (verifiable, persistent, collapse-prone); the e2e map's lifecycle requirements (verify, update in place, forget selectively) are met by neither alone, which is the argument for the side-store hybrid the program already carries as Route C.
