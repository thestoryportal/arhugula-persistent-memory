## 1. What the arc was asked and what it found

The operator ruled on 2026-10-05 that a side store is not F1 closure for C10 and that, before any in-weight route is committed, the program exhausts (1) the literature it already holds, (2) the literature it has not yet reached, and (3) the connections between them. The question that decides the route is narrow: does any published work write a coined, never-seen multi-word value into weights by locate-then-edit and score it on held-out paraphrases, and if not, which mechanism is closest and what is its falsifier here.

**Answer, stated as what was searched rather than as a property of the literature:** in the twenty-three papers read at the evaluation section (§3), in the 243 logged arXiv hits over fifteen queries (`part2/_arxiv_pass_log.json`), and in the eight InfraNodus scientific-import graphs (§4), **no paper was found** that writes a coined multi-token name into weights by locate-then-edit and scores held-out paraphrase recall. The field scores "generalization", "paraphrase" and "portability" on real or counterfactual entities (CounterFact, zsRE, MQuAKE, S2RKE, UnKEBench, RippleEdits). The closest work is outside locate-then-edit: *Fresh in memory* (fine-tunes Llama-3.2-1B on 16,000 entities whose names are replaced by unique three-token random aliases such as `sjdhf` and five-token phrases such as `prickly cyan mouse`, probes held-out entities and an unseen QA template, §2 and Table 1), *Can LMs Learn New Entities from Descriptions?* (real post-cutoff entities by fine-tuning or MEND, Table 3), and from pass 3 *Synthetic Worlds* (fictional future-world events written by mid-training and instruction tuning, abstract only) and MEGa (event memories in gated per-memory low-rank weights selected by query-embedding matching; fictional characters; abstract only). All four are fine-tuning or adapter routes; they are calibration material for the admission test, not solutions.

What the arc found is nine mechanism ingredients the program's knob sweeps never touched, two admission-test predictors, and one diagnostic. **Cheapness, corrected after advisor review:** only one ingredient runs on the unmodified engine from configuration alone (I-9a, a sparse band); I-8 is a science-path change carried in data (it changes the solve's regulariser) and is handled under LAW#5 discipline even though no source line changes; I-1 and I-2 replace or bypass `compute_z` and are science path outright.

## 5. Part 3 — convergence

Merged graph over q1, q3, q4, q8 and the repo-cited arc-1 graph; research-ideas pass (gapDepth 1, several gaps) on q8; conceptual-bridges pass on q4. Outputs are leads; each is checked against the full texts it names.

- **S-1 SPECULATIVE-COMBINATION:** construct the edit target inside the subspace that SUIT and CrispEdit protect, propagate it forward (I-2), weight its tokens (I-1), solve under identity or trace-matched geometry (I-8) at a two-layer set (I-9). No paper combines these. The parts run in isolation in the queue order of §8 so any later combination has single-factor baselines.
- **S-3 (research-ideas pass, q8) — the one sharp unclaimed question, adopted as a pre-registration rule:** null-space/subspace constraints prevent *interference* with old knowledge; they do not prevent *under-generalization* of new knowledge; the literature treats these orthogonal failures as one and the two leading clusters may be in an unmeasured tradeoff. For this program: locality and held-out generalization are scored as **separate axes with separate numeric pass criteria**, and any subspace/null-space ingredient carries the pre-registered prediction that it may *lower* held-out recall. RoSE's locality cost (Tables 1–2) and *Labyrinth and the Thread* (regularisers often unnecessary) are the first literature data points on that tradeoff. Verdict: I-INFER → design rule.
- **S-4 (research-ideas pass, q8):** paraphrase-set preference as the editing objective — the strict held-out bar restated as an objective. Guard: the held-out paraphrase set stays disjoint from anything in the objective; a *training* paraphrase set (OverTone, FE, RoSE's HKI) is admissible. Verdict: I-INFER; no new mechanism; the strict bar binds on the scorer, not the objective.
- **S-5 (conceptual bridges, q4):** same-subject key conflicts as lossy superposition interference with an "elastic recoil" of overwritten facts and a conflict-aware batch-size bound; theory adjoining I-5, I-7 and *Knowledge in Superposition*; bears on C1 and C9, not C10's route.
