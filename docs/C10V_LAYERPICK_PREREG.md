## 1. Question and hypotheses

- **H-V1 (lone-write placement — the claim the design can test):** placing each fact's whole residual at the single band layer where its key's lone-write coefficient `G` is largest raises A7 held-out recall over the fixed band (A), over the same recipe at one fixed layer for all facts (B0), and over the same recipe with the layer assignments shuffled among facts (Bshuf), at the same locality and write size. The selector is credited only if B beats both B0 and Bshuf (§3).
- **H-V0 (null — the prior):** it does not. The band already installs ≈ 97 % of the canonical residual norm (§0), so depth cannot buy much at the canonical position; what remains open is whether a single-layer placement changes the ≈ 23 % remaining on paraphrase prompts or the collateral change, and nothing in CORPUS/37–39, 44 predicts that it does.
- **H-V2 (trade-off):** it realises more but costs the controls (the whole residual at one layer is up to 5 × arm A's per-layer residual, `my_edit:204`), locality, or write size — a strength effect, for which B0 is the control.
- **On LOKI and HSIC.** The stub names HSIC between key and target without a kernel, bandwidth or sample set, and the research pass had only the abstract. The selector below is this program's own and needs no target and no solve at the candidate layers (the stub's step 0 is "minutes"); a key–target dependence statistic needs targets at each candidate layer, one solve per fact per layer. An HSIC form is recorded as a diagnostic using the layer-8 targets.

**Step 0 — the selector, a proxy (no write; minutes).**
- For fact `i` and band layer ℓ ∈ {4, …, 8}: `K_ℓ` = `compute_ks` at ℓ (d × 24), `P_ℓ` the recipe's projector, cache zero, ridge `I`. With `S_ℓ = K_ℓᵀ P_ℓ K_ℓ`, the one-layer solve of `my_edit:205–211` gives `ΔW K_ℓ = R G_ℓ` with **`G_ℓ = S_ℓ (I + S_ℓ)⁻¹`** (24 × 24). For one request, `G = ‖P_ℓ k_i‖² / (1 + ‖P_ℓ k_i‖²)`.
- **What `G_ℓ[i, i]` is:** the coefficient of residual `r_i` at its own context-averaged key under a lone write at ℓ on the pristine model. It is **not** the realised fraction at the canonical prompt or downstream: the realised fraction ⟨Δh, r_i⟩/‖r_i‖² also carries `Σ_{j≠i} G_ℓ[j, i] ⟨r_j, r_i⟩/‖r_i‖²` (target-dependent), B solves with group-only keys (its `G` differs), and the weight is applied in fp16. `G` is a **selection proxy**; what it predicts is measured (readouts below).
- **Rule:** `ℓ*(i) = argmax_ℓ G_ℓ[i, i]`, subject to a margin: the winner counts only if `G_ℓ*[i, i] − G_runner-up[i, i] ≥ δ`, where δ is **measured**, not chosen: step 0 is computed twice in the process (forward passes on MPS, solves on CPU) and δ = the largest absolute change in any `G_ℓ[i, i]` between the two computations; a fact whose margin is below δ takes the layer nearest 8 (the band's z-layer). The margined map must be identical across the two computations, else **"map not reproducible"** and the arm is not run.
- **Recorded, whether or not the arm runs:** the 24 × 5 `G` table, `‖P_ℓ k_i‖` per cell, the winning margin per fact, the runner-up, δ, the context-template list and its hash, and `max_ℓ G_ℓ[i, i]` against the band's **installed share** `1 − ρ_c` (band 0.972; per subject `1 − rho_c_band_per_subject`, `step0.py:136`) — a reported comparison, not a gate (§7, A-0).
- **Stop rule:** if `ℓ*` takes one value on ≥ 20/24 facts the arm is not run and the result is logged as **"concentrated map; candidate not advanced"** (an expenditure screen — it permits four differing assignments and its ability to predict usefulness is unverifiable). Otherwise the map `{subject → ℓ*}` is written to `results/c10v_layerpick/step0.json`, hashed into the run record, and fixed.
- *Expectation stated now:* the absolute ridge and threshold make `G` track per-layer key scale, so a concentrated map (stop fires) or hair-width variation (absorbed by δ) is the likely outcome; this is recorded before the computation.

**HSIC, recorded as a diagnostic, not a selector (A7 only, in the pass).** n = 24; `X_ℓ` rows = `K_ℓ` columns; `Y` rows = `r_i = z_i − cur_i` at layer 8 (`z_i` from `compute_z` at layer 8 on the pristine model, the `compute_zs(ctx)` precedent `c10m_diag_arms.py:72–77`; `cur_i` per `my_edit:197–201`). Gaussian kernel `exp(−‖x − x′‖²/2σ²)`, σ = median pairwise distance among the 24 rows, separately for `X_ℓ` and `Y`; `H = I − 11ᵀ/n`; `HSIC_b = (n − 1)⁻² tr(HK_XH · HK_YH)`; per-fact `c_i(ℓ) = Σ_j (HK_XH)_ij (HK_YH)_ij`; reported with `argmax_ℓ c_i(ℓ)` and its agreement /24 with `ℓ*`.

**Arms (A7, with A1 and A2 under every recipe).**
- **A** — the baseline recipe, band 4–8, the harness as it stands (same process).
- **B0** — fixed single layer: all 24 facts at layer 8 (pre-registered; the band's z-layer), one group through the wrapper. No single-layer arm has ever run; without B0 a B-vs-A difference reads "one layer vs spread".
- **B** — the per-fact map from step 0.
- **Bshuf** — the map's assignments permuted among subjects preserving group sizes: subject `FICTION[i]` receives `ℓ*(FICTION[(i + 1) mod 24])` (the fixed-shift rule of `c10q_stacked_arms.py:47–48`), targets re-solved at the assigned layers; the number of changed assignments is recorded. Without it a B gain supports the grouped single-layer recipe, not the selector.
- **C** (chosen layer plus neighbour) is **deferred out of v2**: it needs its own comparator, label rules, replication requirement and an explicit cache semantics (§7, A-6/C-5); it will be its own pre-registration if B is PARTIAL after gate A.

**Execution of B0, B and Bshuf from `my_edit` as written.** The engine batches every request of one call through one `hp.layers` (`:183–184, :194`), so a map needs **one solve group per distinct layer set**: `g_ℓ = {requests with assigned layer ℓ}`, taken in **descending order 8, 7, 6, 5, 4**, empty groups skipped.
1. *Targets, pre-solved on the pristine model:* `z_i = compute_z(model, tok, req_i, hp, ℓ(i), ctx)` at each fact's assigned layer; placed in `Z_OVERRIDE` under `(subject, " " + value)` (`:97`, `c10q:50`; consumed at `:185–189`). This keeps the engine's convention that every target is solved before any weight changes (`memit_main.py:101–149` before `:152`).
2. *Per group:* `hp_ℓ` = the base file with `layers = [ℓ]`, every other field unchanged; `P = [P_base[ℓ − 4]]`, indexed from the recipe's `compute_P` output, not recomputed; `cache = [zeros]` fresh (as `:474` gives each arm); `my_edit(hp_ℓ, g_ℓ, P, cache)`. Inside: `z_layer = ℓ` (`:184`); targets from the override; one iteration with `resid = tgt` (`:204`) — the whole residual at one layer; `A` holds only the group's keys (`:209`); the post-edit cache update (`:218–220`) is computed and discarded (no later group touches that layer).
3. *Why descending, and its cost:* an edit at layer ℓ changes **its own block output and all deeper activations**; the inputs to layer ℓ's `down_proj` and everything shallower are unchanged. With deeper groups first, each shallower group's `K_ℓ` and `cur_ℓ` are pristine-model reads and its solve equals a lone write's. The trade: shallower groups' ΔW act on every token's `down_proj` input at their layer, so in the evaluated model the deeper groups' keys are no longer the pristine `k_i` their solve assumed — the engine's ascending loop recomputes `cur` after each edit to absorb exactly that (`memit_main.py:152–169`); this design does not. The readouts below measure what that costs.
4. *Mechanism:* `run_recipe` calls `my_edit` by module-global name (`:489`); the driver rebinds `H.my_edit` to a grouped wrapper that delegates unchanged to the original whenever its map is `None` or `H._LAW5_DEFAULT_CTX` is `True` (`:403, :430`); recipe A and the LAW#5 gate run the original path. The driver calls `H.run_recipe` once per recipe (A, B0, B, Bshuf) in one process with `C10_RECIPES=candidate` semantics (`:582`). Audit: groups, requests per group, `Z_OVERRIDE_HITS` = non-empty groups per arm.
5. *Implementation-review items:* (a) an explicit harness global beside `Z_OVERRIDE` (`:407`) versus the rebinding; (b) override keys built per group (`:186` requires every key present); (c) the projector index `ℓ − 4` checked by the numeric audit, not by counters.

**Readouts that test the proxy and the premise (one forward pass each, in the composed post-edit model, for A, B0, B, Bshuf; A7 and A1).** Per fact: the remaining ratio at the keyed position on the canonical prompt and on the three held-out prompts (the `rho_c_band_per_subject` / `rho_p_band_per_subject` formulas, `step0.py:134–137`, with `z` at each fact's assigned layer for the B family and at layer 8 for A, from the driver's pristine-model solves); the realised fraction ⟨Δh, r_i⟩/‖r_i‖² at the canonical keyed position against `G[i, i]` (pristine 24-key `G` and the group-only `G` B actually solved with — both recorded as predictions before the write); the per-layer ΔW norms. A1's ratios answer the advisor's open question (whether realisation at the keyed position separates A1 from A7 at all — never measured).

**Controls and probes.** A1 and A2 under every recipe. Per-frame bystander probe: `frame_probe` over the six never-edited subjects (`c10p_twopass_arms.py:30, :36–42`; flips /6), canonical frame only. Relation-label probes: `"{s} is associated with"` is the bystander (top-1 pre/post, flips counted); `"{s} is a"` is the solve's KL prompt (`C10S:22`, `compute_z.py:127–128`) and is kept but labelled **objective-anchored**, not a clean bystander. Neither exists in the harness (search of `experiments/track_c/*.py`); both are read-only driver additions.

**Inertness (LAW#5), numeric.** (i) The standing gate with the map `None` (`:444`) → INERT. (ii) *Floor:* the original path `my_edit(hp, 24 requests, P, cache)` run twice from identical restored weights, the same `ctx`, `P` and targets (targets pinned through `Z_OVERRIDE` so both runs aim at the same z); the floor = the largest per-layer relative distance ‖ΔW_a − ΔW_b‖/‖ΔW_a‖ and the difference in `canon_full`/`para_full`; tolerance = 10 × floor and never below 1e-4 relative (the C10S §2 rule). (iii) *Active identity map:* the wrapper with every fact assigned the full band `[4..8]` (one group) against the original path on identical weights, contexts, `P` and targets — update tensors within tolerance, behavioural outputs equal. (iv) *Single-layer path:* one request, map `{s → 8}` through the wrapper against `my_edit` with `layers = [8]` on the original path, the engine's own z in the override — within tolerance (this catches a wrong projector index). (v) *Multi-group:* the B map restricted to its two largest groups through the wrapper, against each group written alone from independently restored weights — each layer's ΔW within tolerance of the lone write's (the descending-order premise, tested). ## 3. Pre-registered readings (exact counts; decision order fixed)

**Decision order:** validity first (INVALID ends the reading for that recipe); then the count; then the locality and write-size gates; then the label; then attribution; then replication.

**Validity (any failure → INVALID for that recipe):** fingerprint d941a9fb and `_cov_cpu == 3`; LAW#5 (i)–(v) INERT; map reproduced in-process; A1 ≥ 68 and A2 ≥ 68 **under that recipe** (a control failure is INVALID, never TRADEOFF); recipe A A7 `para_full` in [5, 18]/72 and canon in [5, 10]/24; `Z_OVERRIDE_HITS` = non-empty groups per arm; map hash, `G` table, δ and template hashes in the run record; `para_full_string` = `para_full`.

**Binding count:** B's A7 `para_full` /72 (B0 and Bshuf read by the same rules as their own candidates), per held-out template (3 × 24), per-fact 24-vector with the per-edge rule (pass iff 3/3) and the assigned layer per fact.
- **LEAD** if B's count ≥ 30/72 and every locality gate holds.
- **PARTIAL** if B's count > 18/72 (outside the identical-baseline envelope), B's count − A's ≥ 10/72, B's count < 30, and every gate holds. A count inside [5, 18] is NULL whatever the gain.
- **TRADEOFF** if a PARTIAL- or LEAD-sized gain comes with a locality or write-size gate miss (controls are validity, above).
- **NULL** otherwise (this configuration not advanced).

**Locality gates (vs recipe A, same process):** L1 ≤ A + 1 (of 12); same-frame fictional ≤ A + 1 (of 6); `described_as` ≥ A − 3; L3 ≥ 29/30; generic perplexity ≤ A + 0.5; relation-label flips reported (no threshold in the contract). **Write-size qualifier:** Σ over groups and layers ‖ΔW_B‖ against Σ over layers ‖ΔW_A‖ above 1.5 adds **-LARGE-WRITE**; a LARGE-WRITE label contradicts H-V1's "same write size" and **does not pass gate A**.

**Attribution (the selector is credited only here):** a LEAD or PARTIAL on B carries the label for the selector only if B's count exceeds **both** B0's and Bshuf's by ≥ 10/72 (the contract's gain margin); otherwise the label carries **-PLACEMENT-NOT-ATTRIBUTED** (it belongs to the grouped single-layer recipe, or to B0 if B0 itself labels). **Replication (gate A):** the second process must satisfy every validity and locality gate and the same count rule, else **-NOT-REPLICATED** and nothing is adopted. The historical envelope and two processes do not give a calibrated false-positive rate; the labels buy or decline a next step, nothing more. **Reported, not gated:** the realised count by assigned layer; Spearman of `G[i, i]` at the assigned layer against the per-fact outcomes; the proxy's prediction error on the canonical activation change (fp16 application noted).

PARTIAL on B, attributed and replicated → arm C as its own pre-registration.
