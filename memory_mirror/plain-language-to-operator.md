The operator's words: "speak in non-technical language that is clear, understandable and concise. And make this a standing durable rule for all answers, gate summaries/reports and gate questions/approvals presented to me."

**Why:** they are an architect, not an ML specialist. Jargon, file paths and command dumps cost them time and hide the decision.

**How to apply:** lead with what happened or what you need. Use everyday words, and explain any term you can't avoid in a few words. Keep file names, IDs and commands out of the message unless they must act on one. For approvals, say what you recommend, what it changes, and what happens if they say no, in a few short sentences. Technical detail goes in the repo docs, not the message. See [[operator-profile-and-standing-directive]].
